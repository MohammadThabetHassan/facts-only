# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

The goal of FactLens is to make information harder to manipulate. That work
requires participation from people across every political, national and
religious line. Harassment, personal attacks, and advocacy dressed up as
"verification" undermine that goal and are not tolerated.

Examples of behavior that creates a welcoming environment:

- Critiquing code, data and arguments — not people, identities or nationalities.
- Assuming good faith across political differences; this project's contributors
  and users will disagree strongly about the topics it is used on.
- Accepting that neutrality is a process (open code, cross-partisan heuristics,
  public tests), not a claim about the truth of any side.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Trolling, insults, slurs, or sexualized language.
- Sustained political campaigning in issues, PRs or chats.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Pushing code or "heuristics" designed to favor one side of a dispute.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code,
and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for serious or repeated
violations. Report problems via GitHub's private contact options for this
repository. Reports are reviewed confidentially.

This code applies within all project spaces (code, issues, PRs, chats) and when
an individual officially represents the project.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
